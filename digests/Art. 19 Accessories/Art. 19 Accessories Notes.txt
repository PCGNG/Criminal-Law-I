An accessory does not know the criminal design of the principal concurred in by an accomplice. What the accessory knows is that a crime has been committed, and despite having knowledge that a crime has been committed, still, he participated after the commission of the crime. He takes part subsequent to the commission of the crime. Hence, his participation is after the fact, after the commission of the crime.

### ACTS OF AN ACCESSORY 
First act - By profiting themselves or assisting the offender to profit by the effects of the crime  
Either the accessory himself profited or he assisted the principal to profit from the effects or proceeds of the crime.

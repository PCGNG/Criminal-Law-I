### That the crime be committed in the palace of the Chief Executive or in his presence, or where public authorities are engaged in the discharge of their duties, or in a place dedicated to religious worship.

In the fifth paragraph of Art. 14, all of these are places. These are places which the law requires to be respected but instead of respecting the place, the offender committed a crime in this place, therefore, it shows greater criminality and perversity on the part of the offender.

If the crime is committed in the palace of the Chief Executive, it will always be aggravating even though there is no social or political function involved.

If it is committed in the presence of President Bongbong Marcos, it will always be another aggravating circumstance. He is the highest man of the land. He ought to be respected.

If the crime is committed in the place where public authorities are discharging their duties, it is necessary that at the time of the commission of the crime, these public authorities are actually performing or discharging their duties in order to show disrespect.

If the crime is committed in a place devoted to religious worship, such as the mosque of the Muslim, church of the Iglesia ni Kristo, church of Aglipayan, and Catholic Church- all places of worship. If the crime is committed in these places, it shows greater criminality of the said offender. He could have committed the crime outside, but he chose the said places. It shows greater criminality.

### QUESTION 1:
There was a cabinet meeting in Malacanang. Everyone was there, including all cabinet secretaries. The waiters were serving lunches to the cabinet secretaries. X, a newly hired waiter, was so nervous that the spoon and fork fell on the floor. It caused a disturbance that everybody stopped in the meeting. X felt ashamed and apologized. Thereafter, X went to the kitchen and the head waiter scolded him. The head waiter stated bad and foul words against X. The head waiter was about to stab X, when suddenly X pulled out a knife and stabbed him. The head waiter sustained a non-fatal wound. X is now being prosecuted for attempted homicide. In the commission of the crime, is the aggravating circumstance that the crime was committed in the palace of the Chief Executive present?
### **ANSWER**
NO. X did not deliberately seek the place to commit the crime. It cannot be said that he disrespected the said place because the act of stabbing happened in the spur of the moment, when he was being scolded and embarrassed by the head waiter. Therefore, it cannot be considered as an aggravating circumstance.

### QUESTION 2:
X and his family were hearing the mass inside the church. When Y entered the church, he saw X and seated next to X. When it was about time to say, "peace be with you", Y greeted X. X was shocked upon seeing Y, but he nevertheless stated, "peace be with you" to him. However, at that particular moment, Y stabbed X. Is an aggravating circumstance present in this case?
### **ANSWER**
YES. In the commission of the crime the aggravating circumstance that the crime was committed in a place devoted to religious worship is present. Y could have waited X to go outside and stab him outside - not inside the church, not inside the place of religious worship, not inside a place of prayer. It shows greater criminality. Therefore, it will be an aggravating circumstance.

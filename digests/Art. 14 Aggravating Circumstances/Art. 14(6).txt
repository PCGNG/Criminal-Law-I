### That the crime be committed in the night time, or in an uninhabited place, or by a band, whenever such circumstances may facilitate the commission of the offense.  
Whenever more than three armed malefactors shall have acted together in the commission of an offense, it shall be deemed to have been committed by a band.

In paragraph 6, there are three aggravating circumstances: 
      1. Nighttime 
      2. Uninhabited place, and 
      3. By a band.  

Nighttime is considered as an aggravating circumstance when the following elements are present: 
      1. That the offender deliberately sought the cover of darkness, and 
      2. The purpose of the offender is to ensure the commission of the crime, or to ensure or avoid impunity.

The offender deliberately sought the darkness of the night to ensure that he will be able to consummate the crime without any interference or anyone stopping him from doing it; or he deliberately sought the cover of darkness to ensure that his identity will not be revealed, or his identity will be concealed because the darkness of the night. Nighttime shall be considered as an aggravating circumstance.
Even if the offender sought the cover of darkness in order to ensure the commission of the crime, if at the time of committing the crime, the scene was illuminated by any light, then nighttime is no longer an aggravating circumstance.
According to the SC, lights coming from nearby houses, passing vehicles, lamps, streetlights, or moon shining brightly - if all of these illuminated the scene of the crime, nighttime is no longer present as an aggravating circumstance.
An Uninhabited place is considered as an aggravating circumstance when: 
      1. In the place where the crime was committed, there was little or remote possibility for the victim to receive help, and
      2. The offender deliberately took advantage of the said place in order to ensure or facilitate the commission of the crime.
The mere fact that the place is far from town or other houses, that alone will not suffice in order for an uninhabited place to be considered as an aggravating circumstance. It is necessary in that place - was that very little or remote possibility for the victim to be given help? Did the offender deliberately seek the place to ensure the commission of the crime? If the answer is yes, then an uninhabited place is present as an aggravating circumstance.
A band is considered an aggravating circumstance when more than three armed malefactors shall have acted together in the commission of the crime. Therefore, there must be at least four armed malefactors who should have acted together in the commission of the crime. They all participated in the commission of the crime. They are co- conspirators in the commission of the crime.

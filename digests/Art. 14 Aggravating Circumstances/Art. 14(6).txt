### That the crime be committed in the night time, or in an uninhabited place, or by a band, whenever such circumstances may facilitate the commission of the offense.  
Whenever more than three armed malefactors shall have acted together in the commission of an offense, it shall be deemed to have been committed by a band.

In paragraph 6, there are three aggravating circumstances: 
      1. Nighttime 
      2. Uninhabited place, and 
      3. By a band.  

Nighttime is considered as an aggravating circumstance when the following elements are present: 
      1. That the offender deliberately sought the cover of darkness, and 
      2. The purpose of the offender is to ensure the commission of the crime, or to ensure or avoid impunity.

The offender deliberately sought the darkness of the night to ensure that he will be able to consummate the crime without any interference or anyone stopping him from doing it; or he deliberately sought the cover of darkness to ensure that his identity will not be revealed, or his identity will be concealed because the darkness of the night. Nighttime shall be considered as an aggravating circumstance.
Even if the offender sought the cover of darkness in order to ensure the commission of the crime, if at the time of committing the crime, the scene was illuminated by any light, then nighttime is no longer an aggravating circumstance.
According to the SC, lights coming from nearby houses, passing vehicles, lamps, streetlights, or moon shining brightly - if all of these illuminated the scene of the crime, nighttime is no longer present as an aggravating circumstance.
An Uninhabited place is considered as an aggravating circumstance when: 
      1. In the place where the crime was committed, there was little or remote possibility for the victim to receive help, and
      2. The offender deliberately took advantage of the said place in order to ensure or facilitate the commission of the crime.
The mere fact that the place is far from town or other houses, that alone will not suffice in order for an uninhabited place to be considered as an aggravating circumstance. It is necessary in that place - was that very little or remote possibility for the victim to be given help? Did the offender deliberately seek the place to ensure the commission of the crime? If the answer is yes, then an uninhabited place is present as an aggravating circumstance.
A band is considered an aggravating circumstance when more than three armed malefactors shall have acted together in the commission of the crime. Therefore, there must be at least four armed malefactors who should have acted together in the commission of the crime. They all participated in the commission of the crime. They are co- conspirators in the commission of the crime.

### QUESTION:
X went to the public market. It was already 6:00PM. After buying goods needed by his family, he boarded the jeepney enroute to his house. The jeepney was still waiting for it to be full capacity. Y arrived and seated next to X. The moment the jeepney was full, it already left, and stopped at a gas station which is the end of its route. Thereafter, all passengers alighted. X was walking about 20 meters towards her house. Unknown to her, she has been followed by Y. That was past 7:00PM. Y suddenly grabbed her and covered her mouth. Thereafter, he forcibly dragged her to a creek, wherein there was a small body of water and no houses, but only grasses and tall grasses in the said area. Y had carnal knowledge with X by means of force and violence. In the commission of the crime, are the aggravating circumstances of nighttime and uninhabited place present?
*ANSWER*
Nighttime is NOT PRESENT. There was no showing that Y deliberately sought the cover of darkness to ensure the commission of the crime. It just so happened that the jeepney reached the place at 7:00PM. Therefore, Y, not having sought the cover of darkness in the commission of the crime, then nighttime is not to be appreciated as an aggravating circumstance.  
An uninhabited place is PRESENT. Y deliberately dragged X to a creek, near a small body of water and there were no houses, but only trees and grasses. There and then, he had carnal knowledge with X. In the said place, there was little or remote possibility for X to be given help. Y obviously deliberately sought that place in order to ensure the act of rape.

---
### QUESTION:
 In the same problem, was there abuse of superior strength?
*ANSWER*
NO. Abuse of superior strength is inherent in the act of rape. Therefore, it cannot be considered as an aggravating circumstance.

### QUESTION
Q: X was boarding at Dapitan. At past 9:00PM, X went out because he needed to buy a thing needed for his project. He was about to go back to his boarding house, when suddenly five men, A, B, C, D, and E, crowded him. Without any warning, A, B, C, D, and E suddenly boxed, hit, mauled, and punched him everywhere on the different parts of his body. They only stopped when the barangay chairman arrived. Thereafter, A, B, C, D, and E were arrested. In the commission of the crime, are the aggravating circumstances of nighttime and a band present?  A: Nighttime is NOT PRESENT. A, B, C, D, and E did not seek the cover of darkness to ensure the commission of the crime. It is a matter of fact that Dapitan at 9:00PM is still very, very lighted. It cannot be considered as a dark place. Therefore, nighttime cannot be considered.  A band is NOT PRESENT. Even though there are five men who mauled X, there was no mention in the problem that they were armed. For a band to be considered, the law requires that there must be at least four armed malefactors who shall have acted together in the commission of the crime. In the problem, A, B, C, D, and E punched and boxed him, but there was no mention that they were armed. Therefore, there is no band as an aggravating circumstance.

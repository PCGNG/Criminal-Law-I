### That the act be committed with abuse of confidence or obvious ungratefulness. 
#### Elements of Abuse of Confidence
1. That the offended party has trusted the offender;
2. That the offender has violated, abused the trust and confidence reposed on him;  
3. The said abuse of trust and confidence facilitated the commission of the crime. 
#### UNGRATEFULNESS
For it to be considered as an aggravating circumstance, it must be obvious, apparent, or evident, otherwise, it will not be appreciated as an aggravating circumstance.

---
### QUESTION 1:
X wanted to take a revenge on Y. However, X could not penetrate the conjugal house of Y because it was so big, so thick with glasses and concrete. So, he conducted a surveillance on Y and he learned that every Friday night, instead of going to the conjugal house, Y will go to the house of his mistress. And in the house of his mistress, he will be staying there until Sunday night. After which, he will be going back to the conjugal house. And so, what X did in order to easily kill Y, he waited for him in the house of the mistress. As early as 6 o'clock, he was already there waiting for the arrival of Y. Around 8 o'clock in the evening, Y arrived while X was in hiding. Y went upstairs where he and the mistress kissed and embraced each other. The moment they closed the door, X suddenly barged inside and without any warning, repeatedly stabbed Y resulting to his death. In the commission of the crime, is dwelling aggravating?
### **ANSWER**
YES. Dwelling is an aggravating circumstance. To appreciate dwelling as an aggravating circumstance, ownership is immaterial. Therefore, even if Y is not the owner of the said dwelling, even if the said dwelling is owned by the lessors, and the fact that he used the place for rest and comfort from Friday night to Sunday night, it is within the meaning of dwelling. When Y was still there, dwelling is an aggravating circumstance. X violated the privacy of Y's abode.

### QUESTION 2:
X was a helper of couple Y and Z. He is a stay-out helper for two years. His hours of work are 5 o'clock in the morning until 5 o'clock in the afternoon. It was Saturday, Y and Z, together with their grandchildren, decided to go to the mall. Y and Z told X, "If you are done with the household chores, if it is not yet 5 o'clock, you may already leave. Just make sure that all doors and windows are closed and locked." Thereafter, Y and Z and their grandchildren left. Hours later, X called his two friends, A and B. A and B arrived. A and B were armed with axes and X allowed them to enter. And once inside, all of them A, B, and C ransacked the said house by breaking all the doors, all the locked cabinets, and took all the jewelry, cash, and anything of value. Once they were done, they were about to leave but X heard the arrival of the car of Y and Z. They were afraid that they might be discovered so they all hid. The moment Y and Z, together with their grandchildren, entered the house, they were shocked to see everything in disarray and broken. They were about to ask help from the people when suddenly X, A and B came out and killed Y and Z and the grandchildren. Y and Z, at that time, were both 70 years old, while the grandchildren were 9 years old and 10 years old. In the commission of the crime, are the aggravating circumstances of disregard of: (a) rank, (b) age, (c) dwelling and (d) abuse of confidence present?
### **ANSWER**
(a)(b) NONE. There is no disregard of rank and no disregard of age for two reasons:  
1. Disregard of rank and disregard of age can only be appreciated in Crimes against Persons under Title VIII and in Crimes against Honor under Title XIII. The crime committed by X, A, and B is the special complex crime of Robbery with Homicide which is a Crime against Property under Title X. Therefore, since it is a Crime against Property under Title X, disregard of rank and disregard of age cannot be considered.  
2. In the commission of the crime based on the facts of the case, there was no showing that in killing Y, Z and the grandchildren, X, A and B had the deliberate intent to insult, disregard and disrespect the senior age and the minor age of the victims. Therefore, for these reasons, disregard of rank and disregard of age cannot be considered in this case. 

(c) YES. Dwelling can be considered in the special complex crime of Robbery with Homicide. Dwelling is only inherent in the crime of Robbery with use of force upon things under Art. 299. But dwelling is not inherent in the crime of Robbery with violence against or intimidation of persons under Art. 294, which includes the special complex crime of Robbery with Homicide. Robbery with Homicide can be committed even outside the dwelling of the victim, hence, if it is committed inside the dwelling, it will be an aggravating circumstance.

(d) YES. Abuse of confidence can be appreciated BUT ONLY INSOFAR AS X IS CONCERNED, it will not apply to A and B because the offended parties, Y and Z, only trusted X. But X abused the trust and confidence reposed on him being the houseboy/house helper. The said abuse of trust and confidence facilitated the commission of the crime. Had not Y and Z trusted X, they would not have left X alone in the house. But because of the trust and confidence, they allowed X to be left alone in the house; and that facilitated the commission of the crime. Once X was alone, he called A and B and they committed the crime of Robbery. Therefore, in this case, disregard of rank and disregard of age cannot be appreciated, dwelling can be appreciated and abuse of confidence can be appreciated only insofar as X is concerned as an aggravating circumstance.

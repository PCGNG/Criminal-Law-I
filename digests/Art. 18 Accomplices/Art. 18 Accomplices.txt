### ELEMENTS BEFORE ONE CAN BE HELD LIABLE AS AN ACCOMPLICE
1. That there exists a community of design;
2. That the offender performs acts previous or simultaneous to the commission of the crime; and
3. That there is direct connection between the acts done by the principal and those attributed to an accomplice.

There is a community of design the moment the accomplice concurs with the criminal design authored by the principal, and after concurring with the criminal design authored by the principal, the accomplice performs acts previous or simultaneous  to the commission of the crime which are directly related to the acts performed by the principal.

---
### PRINCIPAL BY INDISPENSABLE COOPERATION
A principal by indispensable cooperation is a co-conspirator. Without his act, the crime will not be consummated or accomplished. Hence, a principal by indispensable cooperation is a co-conspirator.

### ACCOMPLICE
An accomplice is not a co- conspirator. The act of an accomplice is not indispensable in order to consummate the crime. With or without the performance of the act by an accomplice, the crime nevertheless be consummated. His act is minor in character; he merely provides moral aid or support on the part of the said principal.

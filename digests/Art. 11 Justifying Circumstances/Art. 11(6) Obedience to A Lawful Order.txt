ARTICLE 11. Justifying circumstances. - The following do not incur any criminal liability:  
xxxx
6. Any person who acts in obedience to an order issued by a superior for some lawful purpose. 
xxxx
*Elements of Art. 11(6) – Obedience to a Lawful Order*
1. That an order has been issued by a superior; 
2. The order must be lawful; and 
3. The means used by the subordinate to carry out the order must also be lawful.
Based on these elements, it is not only necessary that the order coming from the superior must be lawful; it is also necessary that the means used by the subordinate to carry out the order must also be lawful.

### QUESTION:
X was convicted by the RTC of the crime of Murder. X is now biyaheng Muntinlupa. He was on this vehicle of the BJMP. There was a driver, there was BJMP personnel, there were two police officers, and there was X inside the vehicle. When they were already nearing Muntinlupa, X suddenly told the officers that he wanted to pee. When the officers told him, "No, within 15 minutes, we will already be in Muntinlupa. You do your act there." X said, "if you will not do it, I will pee here." Because of that, the police officer told the driver to stop the vehicle and then thereafter, one of the police officers, Y, accompanied X. So, Y accompanied X behind the tree. X told Y, "Paki-bukas po ng zipper ko, naka-handcuffs ako." The police officer felt degraded, so what he did was he opened the handcuffs on the hands of X. The moment his handcuffs were unlocked, X boxed and kicked police officer Y. Thereafter, X ran away. Police officer Z and the BJMP personnel, all chased X.
X went in a community, in a neighborhood. The people on the neighborhood knew he was an escapee because he was wearing an orange shirt with the letter "P." They told the police officers, "Doon po pumunta, doon po sa bahay na yun." So, police officers X and Z, and the BJMP personnel went to the said house and called on X to come out. However, when X came out, he was already holding a pregnant woman as hostage. A bolo was placed on the neck of the said pregnant woman. The neck of the pregnant woman was already bleeding. X told the police officers, "If you will not allow me to leave, I will stab this pregnant woman. It is her life or mine." X was trying to walk, holding a knife against the neck of this pregnant woman held by him as hostage, when suddenly the said pregnant woman struggled and kicked X. At that particular moment, police officer Y fired a shot at X hitting him on the head. X instantly died.
Police officer Y is now being prosecuted for the crime of Homicide. If you are the counsel of police officer Y, what defenses will you raise in order to free your client Y from criminal and civil liability?
*ANSWER*
You can raise FULFILLMENT OF DUTY. All the elements are present.  
*First element:* Police officer Y was acting in the performance of his duty. It was his duty to first, bring this prisoner back to jail; and second, to save the life of this pregnant woman.  
*Second element:* The said resulting injury, that is the death of X, was the unavoidable consequence of the police officer's performance of duty. Had he not fired at X, the said woman might have been killed.  
Both elements are present, therefore, police officer Y merely acted in the fulfillment of his duty, he should be acquitted of the crime charged. His act is justified.  
As a counsel, you could also raise DEFENSE OF A STRANGER. All the elements are present.  
*First element:* There is unlawful aggression coming from X, there was a knife pointed at the neck of the pregnant woman.  
*Second element:* Reasonable necessity of the means employed to avoid or repel the unlawful aggression, the means used by Y was reasonable. Had he not shot X, X could have killed the said woman.  
*Third element:* Based on the facts of the case, there was no showing that police officer Y was ignited by any evil motive, only a motive to save the life of the said pregnant woman held as hostage. Therefore, this justifying circumstance may also be raised by the counsel.

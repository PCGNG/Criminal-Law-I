3. Anyone who acts in defense of the person or rights of a stranger, provided that the first and second requisites mentioned in the first circumstance of this article are present and that the person defending be not induced by revenge, resentment, or other evil motive.

### Elements of Art. 11(3) - Defense of A Stranger
1. Unlawful Aggression;
2. Reasonable necessity of the means employed to prevent or repel it; and
3. That the person defending be not induced by revenge, resentment, or any other evil motive.

### QUESTION:
The group of students from the University Belt planned to stage a rally at Liwasan, Bonifacio. They applied for a permit. However, City Mayor Lacuna of Manila denied their application for a permit to hold a rally at Liwasan, Bonifacio. Nevertheless, these students proceeded with the said rally. They went from the University Belt, and walked towards Liwasan, Bonifacio. The moment they were already approaching the rally site, these students were met by police officers who were already there. These police officers were armed with truncheon, water hose, all other things, and they began attacking, they began arresting the said students. When one of the students, W, saw a policeman striking X, a fellow student with his truncheon, the said student W hit the forearm of the police officer with an empty bottle of Coke in order to prevent the police officer from further hitting the head of his fellow student with his truncheon. Other police officers came, and arrested W.  
W was charged with Direct Assault. W raised that he was merely acting in defense of a stranger. Are all the elements for defense of a stranger present or is W liable for direct assault?
*ANSWER*
W is NOT LIABLE for direct assault. He merely acted in DEFENSE OF A STRANGER. All the elements are present.
*First element:* 
There was unlawful aggression. The police officer was hitting the head of X with a truncheon, that placed the life and limb of X in imminent danger.  
*Second element:*
Reasonable necessity of the means employed to prevent or repel the unlawful aggression. What W used was only an empty bottle of Coke. W only hit the arm of the police officer with an empty bottle of coke in order to prevent him from further hitting a fellow student with a truncheon. The means used are reasonable. *Third element:* 
That the person defending be not induced by revenge, resentment, or other evil motive. Based on the facts of the case, there was no showing that W was ignited by any evil motive, his only intention was to help a fellow student who was being attacked on the head with a police truncheon.  
All the elements are present. Therefore, defense of a stranger will lie in favor of W. He should be acquitted of the crime of direct assault.
